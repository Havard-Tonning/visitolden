Olden Fjordhotell

Every day the whole summer, Olden Fjordhotel serves a tasty buffet right by the fjord from 19:00 to 22:00. Here, you can enjoy fish, chicken, meat, soup of the day, salad and delicious desserts. The price is 450kr for adults and 175 for children 3-12 years. The also have white and red wine, prosecco and local beer on tap.

You do not need to reserve a table beforehand.
