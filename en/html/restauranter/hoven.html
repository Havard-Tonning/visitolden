Hoven Restaurant

Marius Beck Dahle/Loen Skylift
Marius Beck Dahle/Loen Skylift

With panoramic views right on the edge of the cliff 1011 m above sea level, you'll find Hoven Restaurant. Here you'll be served food with local ingredients from a varied à la carte menu consisting of lunch and dinner dishes, as well as juicy burgers and Italian-inspired pizzas. The menu varies according to season. Enjoy a lunch after the mountain hike, a three-course dinner or just something refreshing in your glass in the sofa area by the bar, or on the terrace on a warm day.

To reach Hoven Restaurant you must take Loen Skylift, walk one of the trails up here or climb Via Ferrata Loen.

There are seats for approximately 370 guests distributed across two sections. The restaurant is shaped like an amphitheater, so that everyone has a good view over the fjord landscape. Throughout the year, various events take place at Hoven Restaurant. Remember to book a table in advance if you plan to eat at the restaurant.
