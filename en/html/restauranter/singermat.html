Singerheimen

At Singerheimen there is afternoon tea at 13:00 and 15:00 Wednesday to Sunday. Here you have the opportunity to travel back in time in the villa at Singerheimen, where everything has been preserved in the authentic style from the mid-20th century.

Here you are served rolls with local cured ham and smoked salmon, scones with delicious raspberry jam, and sweet cakes. And of course tea (or coffee). All the bread and cakes are made in house, and the ingredients are produced locally. The food is served on 100-year-old tableware, which reflects the way Singerheimen is run - conservation through use.

You will find Singerheimen up on the hill just outside the city center. The large green houses are hard to miss.
