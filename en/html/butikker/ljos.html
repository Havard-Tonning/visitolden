Ljós

Ljós fills a gap in an otherwise saturated market of souvenir shops in Olden - here there is a focus on high quality handmade souvenirs. Here they have beautiful prints and postcards with hand-drawn motifs from Norway. They also have scented candles, oils and pins.

Open 10:00-17:00 on cruise ship days
