Bunnpris

Bunnpris Olden can be found in the heart of the village. The grocery store has been here for several generations, and today it is the largest grocery store in the village. The first thing that meets you when you enter the store is a large and inviting fruit section. In the store you will find most of the food, drinks and accessories you need. If you want a simple lunch, they also have a selection of hot food straight from the grill.
