A place where you can play among the treetops

In the forests above Stryn, you find Hydla Climbing Park. Here, they have 7 different climbing routes with a total of 58 obstacles, including 11 zip-lines. These fit both big and small squirrels. In addition, there is a 12 metre high climbing tower where the brave ones can take the Hydla Leap and experience the feeling of free fall.
