Olden Active

Your gateway to an active stay

It would be a shame to visit Olden and not using the nature it has to offer. With Olden active, you can immerse yourself in the fjord with a trip on a Rib boat or kayaks. The forest can be explored with their mountain bikes, and the valleys with guided electric bike tour. Whichever element you want to connect with, Olden Active helps you on the way.
