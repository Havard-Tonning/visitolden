Norway's easiest summit hike

In Loen there is one of the steepest cable cars in the world. This takes you from fjord to 1010 meters in just 5 minutes

Background

The cable car was opened in 2017 by Queen Sonja. It was the first new cable car in Norway in over 50 years. The cable car is one of the steepest in the world, with a maximum gradient of 60% and a height of up to 165 meters above ground.

Extreme sports

Loen has become one of Norway's most popular places for extreme sports, both for paragliders and base jumpers. Instead of spending the whole day hiking up to the top of the mountain, you can take the cable car up and fly down, then repeat the process. Here you get more time in the air than on the ground.

Opportunities at the top

Taking flight is not the only option from the top. Here you'll find well-maintained hiking trails of all lengths and difficulty levels. In winter, the entire transport route down is converted into a 7-kilometer long ski slope.

After taking in what nature has to offer, you can enjoy a good meal with a view over the entire fjord. You won't find a better view for your dinner.
