Norway's smallest and coldest beach

Refreshing glacier water

The beach, which is located in the heart of the village, has a width of 2.5 meters and a temperature between 5 and 8 degrees.

Background

In 2019, merchant André Opheim put up a sign informing that this is the smallest and coldest beach in the country. Meltwater directly from Briksdal Glacier ensures that the temperature, even on the warmest summer days, stays in single digits. What the beach lacks in temperature it makes up for in exclusivity. The beach has fine white shell sand and a view that few can match.

Popularity

After the sign went up, the beach has become a popular destination among tourists. Some dare to venture in to be able to brag that they have bathed on this exact beach.

A couple of years after opening, André set up a sauna where people can warm up after a cold dip. This has also made the beach a year-round beach, where the extra brave can take a dip in winter.
