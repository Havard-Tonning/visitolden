Beautiful and useful

Laukifossen is a beautiful waterfall that is also used for hydroelectric power

About the waterfall

Laukifossen is a small but beautiful waterfall. Meltwater from the glacier provides a beautiful color palette on the water, with shades from deep blue to turquoise green. The surrounding trees and mountains, as well as the old buildings and wooden bridge make this one of the most beautiful places in the village.

The power plant

Since 1917 there has been a power plant at this waterfall. The annual production is 200 megawatt-hours. To put this in context, this is enough electricity to:

  • Supply 10 households for a year
  • Charge 1.3 million phones
  • Charge 40 million toothbrushes
  • Watch TV for 228 years
  • Drive an electric car 30 times around the earth

Information retrieved from NVE.
