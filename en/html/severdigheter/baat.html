Experience Lodalen from the water

Kjenndal II takes you from Sande in the lower part of Lodalen, all the way to Kjenndalsanden by Kjenndalstova near the end of the valley. The whole trip takes about 4 hours.

About the trip

The boat trip itself is abput an hour each way, with a two hour stop at the end of the valley, where you can enjoy a tasty meal or go for a hike in the wonderful nature.

The boat has a capacity of 100 people, with 100 seats inside, 40 on deck and 20 in the rear. The boat offers trips from mid-May until September. The price is 340kr for a round trip or 220kr for one way. Children aged 6-13 cost 170/85kr.

It's possible to book the boat for private events of over 20 people.
