A magical place for the kids

In Loen, there's a place full of play and fun for both small and bigger children. Here, they can play around with trampolines, climbing, balancing and more.

Play for different ages

Loen Play is split in three zones. Funhouse is a climbing house full of climbing, balancing, zip-lines and slides. "Small trolls" is an are for the youngest, with captivating and challenging opportunities for play and discovery. "Full steam" in the basement has a lot of exciting opportunities for the older kids.

Prices and hours

28th June - 17th August: Open daily 11-19
The rest of the year: Open Saturday and Sunday 11-19
Children 1-2 years: 85kr
Children 3-15: 170kr
