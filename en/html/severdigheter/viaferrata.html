Majestic, challenging and rewarding

Via Ferrata Loen is one of the greatest climbing trails in Europe. Here, you start all the way down by the fjord and quickly gain altitude until you reach the top at 1011 metres.

Almost everyone can climb

Via Ferrata means iron trail and comes from the "trail" that is made up the mountain of wires, attachments, steps and bridges. You are at all points connected to the mountain, so even if you were to lose your footing, you wouldn't fall more than a metre or two. This makes it so that even inexperienced climbers can make it to the top. It is worth noting that you need some endurance to go on this trip, and persons with little experience are recommended to go with a guide.

Loen Active

Loen Active are the ones running the trail. They do the guided tours and rental of equipment. They also rent SUP boards, bikes and kayaks, for those who want to stay a bit more grounded.
