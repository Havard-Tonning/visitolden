Unique and modern

New mini house tha sleeps four on two lofts. Living room with dining/sitting area and TV.

Well-equipped kitchen with dishwasher, oven, hob, fridge/freezer and coffee machine.

The location is rural with a quiet atmosphere. Spectacular views of the Nordfjord and mountains such as Hoven and Skåla.

From May to September, guests have access to a heated swimming pool. The pool is open daily from 3:00 PM to 8:00 PM in July, and beyond by appointment.

The host and his family live in their own house in the area. They have been renting since 2018. They also operate the rental units Olden Fjord Apartments and Olden Studioapartment.
