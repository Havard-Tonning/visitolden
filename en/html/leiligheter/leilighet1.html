Peace of mind and soul

New apartments with living room, bathroom, kitchen and one bedroom. The bedroom sleeps four and two on the sofa bed in the living room. The kitchen is equipped with a stove, oven, dishwasher, kettle, coffee capsule machine, coffee press and everything else you need for cooking. There is a TV in the living room and the apartments have wifi.

From May to September, as a guest, you have access to a heated swimming pool. The pool is open daily from 3:00 PM to 8:00 PM in July, and beyond this by appointment. The apartments and pool area have a sunny patio with a seating area.

There is free parking with the possibility of charging an electric car for a fee. Quiet and great location with beautiful views of the Nordfjord and mountains.

The host lives on the property, in a separate house, with her husband and three girls.
