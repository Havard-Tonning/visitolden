Luxury by the fjord

In our new villa you get the luxury and space to relax. Here you have the entire 120 square meter house to yourself, so you and the whole family can have a good time in your own company. The villa has a magnificent view of the fjord that can be enjoyed from a separate outdoor area with garden furniture.

As a guest you have access to a heated swimming pool from May to September. The pool is open daily from 15:00 to 20:00 in July, and beyond this by arrangement. The apartments and the pool area have a sunny patio with a seating area.

The villa has free covered parking for two cars.

The host lives on the property, in a separate house, with her husband and three girls.
