Peace of mind and soul

Studio apartment in the basement at the front of the house with its own entrance and terrace.

Consists of a living room with an open kitchen and a sleeping alcove. The kitchen is equipped with a stove, oven, dishwasher, kettle, coffee capsule machine and everything else you need for cooking.

Private bathroom with shower and washer/dryer. There is a TV in the living room and the apartment has free wifi.

From May to September, as a guest, you have access to a heated swimming pool. The pool is open daily from 3:00 PM to 8:00 PM in July, and beyond this by arrangement.

There is free parking on the premises, with the possibility of charging electric cars, for a fee.

Very secluded without any privacy. Quiet and great location with a beautiful view. Here you can wake up to spectacular views of the fjord and mountains. The apartment is perfect for a couple children.

Has rented out apartments, Olden Fjord Apartments, since 2018. New studio apartment, Olden Studioapartment, for rent June 2021. Lives on site with husband and three girls.
