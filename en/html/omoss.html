About us

Learn more about who we are!

Family run

Family owned accomodation establishment started in 2018. We are located at the picturesque family farm Vangberg, approximately 4 kilometres from Olden village centre.

The histoy of the farm goes over 1000 years into the past.

Here, you can enjoy quiet surroundings with spectacular views of fjords and mountains.

The hosts, with three children, live on the property.
