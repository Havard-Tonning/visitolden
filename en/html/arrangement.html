Events

The perfect venue, regardless of the event

Do you dream of celebrating a special occasion in idyllic surroundings with a view of the fjord? Then we have exactly what you are looking for. Whether it is a birthday, a wedding, a concert, or something completely different, we will make it happen.

We have tables and tableware with space for up to 70 people. The tent ensures that the event runs smoothly regardless of the weather.

The nearby apartments sleep 18 and provide an ideal opportunity to retreat and get a good night's sleep after the festivities.

Contact us, and we ensure that the special day will be unforgettable
